Provide liquidity only in a market that is going sideways. The week and the 30-day lines must show price going back and forth: a small change over them against the distance between their high and their low, with both visited. Place the range inside the band the 30 days held, {one_sided}, wide enough for the day's volatility, and leave it in place while price stays in that band. When price leaves the band, or the week turns into a run, exit and stay out until it is sideways again. Most looks in a running market should end in `stay_out`. In every `--reasoning`, give the band you see and why the market is or is not sideways.
